# MAXXA AI Anime Voice Pack Update

Added 12 selectable character-inspired TTS presets to the Voice Pack screen: Itachi, Naruto, Sasuke, Kakashi, Madara, Hinata, Sakura, Nezuko, Obito, Minato, Mitsuri, and Nobara.

Each preset has separate Hindi/Hinglish and English preview buttons. The selected character preset is persisted in `maxxa_settings` and applied to assistant speech through Android TextToSpeech pitch and speech-rate settings. The existing reply-language preference remains in use.

These are lightweight character-inspired TTS styles, not original anime actor recordings, voice cloning, or exact reproductions. Actual timbre and language availability depend on the Android device's installed TTS engine.

Validation: source-level consistency checks and ZIP integrity only. Android compilation and physical-device playback testing have not been performed in this environment.
